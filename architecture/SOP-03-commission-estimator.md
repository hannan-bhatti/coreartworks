# SOP-03: Commission & Project Cost Estimator

## 1. Objectives
Provide prospective studio clients, game developers, and commercial buyers with transparent, instant pricing models and brief generation.

## 2. Calculation Logic
Base Rate Formula:
`Estimated Price = (Base Discipline Rate * Scope Multiplier * Complexity Factor * Timeline Urgency Multiplier) + Licensing Fee`

- **Base Discipline Rates**:
  - Concept Art: $800 - $2,500 / key visual
  - 3D Modeling & Sculpting: $1,200 - $3,800 / asset
  - 2D & Editorial Illustration: $900 - $3,000 / piece
  - VFX & Animation: $1,500 - $5,000 / sequence
  - Brand Identity & Game UI: $1,800 - $6,000 / system

- **Timeline Modifiers**:
  - Standard (4-6 weeks): 1.0x
  - Fast-Track (2-3 weeks): 1.25x
  - Rush Sprint (1 week): 1.6x

- **Commercial Rights**:
  - Personal / Indie portfolio: 1.0x
  - Full Commercial & Franchise Buyout: +25%

## 3. Payload Export
- Generates a formatted creative brief object ready to copy or submit directly to the contact intake endpoint.
